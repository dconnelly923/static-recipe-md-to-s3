## Ingredients

- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts, pounded to 1/2 inch thick
- 1/8 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1 cup seasoned breadcrumbs
- 2–3 tbsp olive oil
- 8 slices mozzarella cheese
- 1 jar spaghetti sauce
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated
- 8 oz angel hair pasta

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. Lightly grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish.
3. Whisk together the egg and milk in a bowl.
4. Put the seasoned breadcrumbs in a wide, shallow bowl.
5. Dip the chicken breasts in the egg and milk mixture, then in breadcrumbs, turning to coat thoroughly.
6. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
7. Brown the chicken on both sides until golden, about 3 to 4 minutes per side. Arrange in the prepared baking dish.
8. Place two slices of mozzarella on each chicken breast. Pour the spaghetti sauce over all. Sprinkle with parmesan and a little more mozzarella. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until bubbly.
9. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in boiling salted water following package directions. Drain well.
10. Serve chicken over pasta.
