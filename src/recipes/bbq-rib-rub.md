---
title: BBQ Rib Rub
category: seasoning
date: 2026-02-21
description: A sweet and smoky dry rub with brown sugar, chili powder, and cayenne for ribs and grilled meats.
---

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup salt
- 1/8 cup brown sugar
- 4 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients, breaking up any clumps of brown sugar.
2. Store in an airtight container or use immediately.
