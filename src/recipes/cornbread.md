## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients

- 1/2 cup all purpose flour corn flour if gluten free
- 1/4 cup corn meal
- 1/2 cup sugar
- .5 tsp baking powder
- .5 tsp baking soda
- .25 tsp salt

### Wet Ingredients

- 1/2 cup milk I use 2%
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 2 eggs beaten

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F with an 8-10 inch cast iron skillet inside
2. Combine all dry ingredients in a mixing bowl
3. Add all wet ingredients, then lightly mix until combined, do not over mix
4. Remove preheated skillet from oven and lightly grease
5. Add batter to skillet and smooth out with a rubber spatula
6. Move skillet back to oven and bake for 15-20 minutes. Cornbread is done when a toothpick comes out clean

## Notes

Make sure oven is preheated before adding wet ingredients into dry ingredients. You need to add to oven quickly after mixing due to the baking soda.
