## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 cups tomato sauce
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp steak seasoning
- 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 small bell pepper, chopped
- 4 hamburger buns, split

## Instructions

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add oil and meat to the pan. Spread the meat around the pan and begin to break it up.
3. Add sugar and steak seasoning to the skillet and mix.
4. When the meat has browned, add onion and bell pepper to the skillet and mix.
5. Reduce heat to medium, add red wine vinegar and worcestershire sauce, and cook for 5 more minutes.
6. Add tomato sauce and paste to pan. Stir to combine. Reduce heat to simmer and cook 5 minutes longer.
7. Serve on buns.
