## Equipment

- Smoker

## Ingredients

- 4-5 lb chuck roast
- 2 large bell peppers, sliced
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 3 jalapeños (more or less to adjust heat)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup worcestershire sauce
- 1 can or bottle Guinness extra stout (or other dark lager)
- BBQ beef rub

## Instructions

1. Liberally season chuck roast with BBQ beef rub.
2. Cook on smoker with oak wood at 250°F until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
3. Place remaining ingredients in a 10" x 13" pan as a bed for the meat, then place chuck roast on top.
4. Cover pan tightly with aluminum foil.
5. Place pan in smoker or oven and continue to cook at 350°F until meat is fork-tender (about 200°–205°F internal temp.).
6. Remove pan from cooker and discard foil.
7. Pull/shred meat and mix with vegetables.
8. Return pan to smoker or oven, uncovered, and continue to cook until liquid is reduced by about half.
