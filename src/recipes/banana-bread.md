---
title: Banana Bread
date: 2024-06-15
---

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 1/3 cup melted butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350 F.
2. Mash bananas in a bowl, stir in melted butter.
3. Mix in sugar, egg, and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle in baking soda and salt, then add flour.
5. Bake in a buttered loaf pan for 50 to 60 minutes.
