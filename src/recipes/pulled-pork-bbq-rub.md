---
title: Pulled Pork BBQ Rub
category: seasoning
date: 2026-02-21
description: A sweet and smoky rub with brown sugar, paprika, and rosemary for pulled pork.
---

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup paprika
- 1/4 cup garlic powder
- 2 tbsp salt
- 2 tbsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp ground ginger
- 2 tbsp onion powder
- 2 tbsp rosemary powder

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients.
2. Store in an airtight container or use immediately.
