## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, spatchcocked / split
- 4 tbsp salt
- 2 tbsp ground cumin
- 2 tbsp paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp distilled white vinegar
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Combine salt, cumin, paprika, pepper, garlic, vinegar, and oil in a small bowl and whisk until homogeneous. Spread the mixture evenly over all surfaces of the chicken. Best to marinate for a few hours to overnight, but can be cooked right away.
2. Set up grill for offset cooking at medium heat.
3. Place chicken skin-side up on the cooler side of the grill, with legs facing toward the hotter side. Close the lid and cook until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the breast registers 110°F.
4. Carefully flip chicken and place skin-side down on the hotter side, with breasts pointed toward the cooler side. Press down firmly with a wide, stiff spatula to ensure good contact with the grill grates. Cover and cook until skin is crisp and the thickest part of the breast registers 165°F, about 10 more minutes. If the chicken threatens to burn before temperature is achieved, slide to the cooler side, cover, and continue cooking.
5. Transfer to a cutting board and rest for 5 to 10 minutes. Carve and serve with Peruvian green sauce.
