## Equipment

- Instant Pot

## Ingredients

- 1 cup white rice, rinsed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp chicken bouillon
- 1 tbsp Goya adobo seasoning (or other seasoned salt)
- 1 tbsp dried parsley flakes

## Instructions

1. Add water, chicken bouillon, and butter to the Instant Pot.
2. Turn Instant Pot to simmer mode.
3. Once butter and bouillon begin to dissolve in the warm water, turn off the Instant Pot.
4. Add remaining ingredients and stir to mix.
5. Set Instant Pot to pressure cook for 9 minutes.
6. Let the Instant Pot natural release for 25 minutes.
7. Open lid and stir rice to fluff, scraping off any stuck-on bits at the bottom.
