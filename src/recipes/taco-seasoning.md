## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1.5 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp coriander
- 0.5 tsp cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients.
2. Store in an airtight container or use immediately.
