## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces (or all thighs)
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 1 rib of celery, finely chopped
- 2 oz prosciutto or country ham, chopped
- 3/4 cup dry white wine
- 3/4 cup chicken stock
- 3 tbsp heavy cream, room temperature
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and cook skin-side down to brown and render the fat, about 15 minutes. Flip and cook on the bone side for about 5 minutes. Remove and reserve the chicken.
3. Pour off all but 2 tbsp of the fat from the pan.
4. Add the onion, carrot, celery, and prosciutto and sauté until the onion is browned and vegetables are softened, 8 to 10 minutes.
5. Add the wine and chicken stock to the pan and stir to combine.
6. Return the chicken to the pan. Bring the liquid to a boil, reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook until the chicken is cooked through, 20 to 25 minutes.
7. Remove the chicken. Turn the heat to high and reduce the liquid by half, about 5 minutes (6 to 7 minutes for a thicker sauce).
8. Add the cream, stir to combine, and remove from heat.
9. Transfer chicken to plates, season the sauce with salt and pepper if needed, and spoon it over the chicken. Sprinkle with chopped parsley.
