category: seasoning
date: 2026-02-21
description: A bold, peppery rub for brisket and beef with mustard, garlic, and a touch of cayenne.
---

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 2 tsp mustard powder
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix all ingredients.
2. Store in an airtight container or use immediately.
