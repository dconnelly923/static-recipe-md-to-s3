## Equipment

- 2 bowls
- Medium saucepan
- Mesh strainer (optional)

## Ingredients

- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups ketchup
- 1/2 cup yellow mustard
- 1/3 cup worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup steak sauce
- 1/4 cup molasses
- 1/4 cup honey
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp hot sauce
- 1 cup brown sugar

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, mix the chili powder, black pepper, and salt.
2. In a large bowl, mix the ketchup, mustard, vinegar, worcestershire, lemon juice, steak sauce, molasses, honey, hot sauce, and brown sugar. No need to mix thoroughly.
3. Over medium heat, warm the oil in a large saucepan.
4. Add the onions and sauté until limp and translucent, about 5 minutes.
5. Crush the garlic, add it, and cook for another minute.
6. Add the dry spices and stir for about 2 minutes to extract their oil-soluble flavors.
7. Add the wet ingredients. Simmer over medium heat for 15 minutes with the lid off to thicken.
8. Taste and adjust.
9. Strain if you don't want it lumpy.
10. Best if refrigerated overnight before using.
