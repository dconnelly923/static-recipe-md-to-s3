## Equipment

- Blender or food processor

## Ingredients

- 3 medium jalapeños, roughly chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled
- 1 cup fresh cilantro
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 2 tsp lime juice (about 1 lime)
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp distilled white vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine jalapeños, cilantro, garlic, mayonnaise, sour cream, lime juice, and vinegar in a blender.
2. Blend on high speed, scraping down as necessary, until smooth.
3. With blender running, slowly drizzle in olive oil.
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Sauce will be quite loose at this point but will thicken as it sits.
5. Transfer to a sealed container and refrigerate until ready to use.
